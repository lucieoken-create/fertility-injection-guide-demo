About this guide

This illustrated guide brings supplies, preparation and injection steps into one place. The phases are fictional examples; personal doses, schedules and mixing amounts are left blank.

Use the current plan

Medicines and doses can change during treatment. Keep the latest instructions together in the current phase and retain earlier plans for reference. Reading checks never record an injection.

Device-specific instructions

The steps describe Follistim Pen, Menopur with a Q-Cap, and the prefilled Ganirelix syringe in the linked demonstrations. Source links open only when you choose them. Full device instructions remain part of the guide, including flow checks and administration details that are not reproduced here.
