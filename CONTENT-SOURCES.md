## Boundaries

Public source links open on deliberate click. No source videos are embedded. Personal emails, source records, photographs and original review notes are excluded from this repository. The displayed steps organize source material, and linked full instructions remain necessary for details not reproduced. The sample is not medically validated or a complete standalone administration protocol.
